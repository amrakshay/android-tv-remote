# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent. This is a small hobby project; nobody is being paid, and nobody owes you their weekend.

## What is expected

- Be patient and courteous with people asking questions, including basic ones.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change because of scope rather than quality.
- Assume the other person meant well. Text loses tone.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberately derailing discussions, or
publishing someone's private information.

## Enforcement

Report problems by opening an issue or contacting the maintainer directly. Reports will be handled
privately where that is possible. Maintainers may edit, hide or delete comments, and block people
who do not stop.
